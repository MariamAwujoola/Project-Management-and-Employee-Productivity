# PROJECT MANAGEMENT AND EMPLOYEE PRODUCTIVITY
---
### Project Overview
 The Project Management and Employee Productivity project was designed to evaluate employee efficiency, project performance, and revenue generation. Utilizing Power BI, the analysis involved data cleaning, DAX calculations, and dashboard development to provide insights into key business metrics such as total billable revenue, productivity, and on time completion rates. 

 ### Data Sources 
 This dataset was gathered through survey and internal data collection, focusing on Project performance and employee performance. All data has been cleaned and anonymized for analysis. 

 ### Tools
 Excel - For collection of Data
 Power BI - Power query for deep cleaning and formatting 

 ### Data Cleaning 
 - Duplicate entries were removed to ensure data accuracy.
 - Inconsistent project statuses (e.g "completed" vs "Complete") were standardized.
 - Negative or missing values in "Hours  Worked" were identified and corrected.

   ### DAX Calculations
   - Total Billable Revenue was computed as the sum of billable hours multiplied by the hourly rate, totaling $279.22k.
   - Average Productivity was measured as total hours worked divided by the employee count, yielding a productivity rate 101.36%
   - Total Overdue Hours across departments amounted to 10k hours, indicating significat project delays.

  ## Recommendation

   - Improve On Time Completion Rate: With a completion rate of 33%, it is crucial to implement better project tracking and deadline management.
  - Address Overview Hours: Prioritize delayed projects and allocate additional resources to ensure timely delivery.
  - Enhance Employee Productivity: Provide targeted training and incentives toemployees with lower productivity rates.
  - Optimize Work Allocation: Departments with high overdue hours (e.g, HR, Finance) may require process improvements or additional staffing.
  - Regular Data Audits: Continous monitoring and cleansing of data will maintain accuracy and reliability for future decision making.
    
 
    By implementing these recommendations, the organization can enhance productivity, streamline operations, and maximize revenue potential.
